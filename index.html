💡 How It Works

A fast-paced showdown between human intuition and machine learning. Here are the three roles in every match:

🎨

1. The Drawer

Draws in the air using hand tracking via webcam, or on a white page using Pen mode. Keeps the secret word hidden from the contestant so only they know what to sketch!

👤

2. YOU (Human)

Watches the live drawing and races to guess the word by typing it into the guess box before the 60-second timer expires or the AI model beats you to it.

🤖

3. THE MODEL (AI)

Evaluates the doodle canvas live every 600ms using a convolutional neural network. Wins the round if its top prediction matches the word with at least 70% confidence!

📜 Game Rules

Everything you need to know before stepping into the arena:

1
5 Rounds per Match

Each showdown lasts exactly 5 rounds. Total scores determine the overall match champion.

2
60 Seconds Each

A countdown timer runs for 60 seconds per round. If time runs out with no correct guess, neither side scores.

3
Secret Word for Drawer Only

The word is revealed for 3 seconds before the round starts. The contestant looks away to keep it secret!

4
First Correct Guess Wins

Whoever (YOU or THE MODEL) correctly identifies the drawing first immediately wins the round point.

5
Faster Guess = More Points & Speed Record

Lightning-fast guessing clocks your personal best time and boosts your match average speed.

6
70% AI Confidence Threshold

The AI only answers when it is at least 70% sure. Below 70%, it hesitates and displays "HMM... NOT SURE".

🧠 Meet the AI Model

A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) built in Google Colab and trained on 100,000 Google Quick, Draw! sketches (5,000 per class across 20 categories). The browser extracts the drawing into a 28×28 grayscale tensor and evaluates it live via TensorFlow.js with 97.0% validation accuracy.

Input Format 28 × 28 Grayscale
Categories 20 Doodle Classes
Training Dataset 100,000 Sketches
Validation Accuracy 97.0% Accuracy
Inference Frequency Every 600 ms
Win Threshold ≥ 70% Confidence

🔬 Preprocessing Pipeline (Identical to Colab)

1. Transparent Alpha Channel Readout (640×480)
2. Tight Bounding Box Crop of Active Strokes
3. Square Aspect Ratio with 10% Edge Margin
4. Area-Interpolation Downscale to 28×28 Grayscale
5. Normalized Tensor (0.0 to 1.0) → CNN Softmax Output
